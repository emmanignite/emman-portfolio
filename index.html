About Me

Video Editor
"I turn raw footage into compelling stories."

I'm Emmanuel — an IT graduate who fell in love with the art of editing. Over the past 3 years, I've crafted everything from social hooks to cinematic narratives, always with a focus on rhythm, emotion, and audience retention.


My technical background gives me an edge in workflow efficiency, while my creative eye ensures every frame serves the story. Whether it's a fast-paced commercial or a docu-style piece, I bring a meticulous, detail-driven approach to every project.


I work exclusively with CapCut — leveraging its powerful tools to deliver polished, professional results without overcomplicating the process. Simplicity, speed, and impact define my style.
