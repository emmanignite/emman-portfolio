About Me
THE EDITOR
BEHIND THE CUT

I'm Emmanuel Cacho — a Filipino video editor and graphic designer with 3+ years of professional experience. My work lives at the intersection of storytelling, rhythm, and visual psychology.

I've partnered with ad agencies, independent creators, and brands across niches — from e-commerce to documentary storytelling — delivering edits that look great and drive measurable results.

Every frame is intentional. Every cut has a purpose. Whether it's a 6-second hook or a 20-minute long-form piece, I bring the same obsessive attention to pacing, emotion, and clarity.
